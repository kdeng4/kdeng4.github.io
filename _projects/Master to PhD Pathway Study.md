title: Pathways from Online Master’s in CS to STEM PhD Programs
description: This study explores how a large online master’s program in computer science prepares graduates for STEM PhD programs. By analyzing enrollment data, surveys, and interviews, the research highlights the program's positive impact on alumni PhD experiences. Key findings include the importance of graduate research and coursework in PhD preparation. The results demonstrate that affordable, online, asynchronous programs can effectively support non-traditional students in pursuing doctoral education. Recommendations are provided for similar programs to enhance research opportunities for PhD-bound students.
